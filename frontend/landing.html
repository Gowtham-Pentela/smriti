Privacy Architecture

Your data never leaves
your cloud. Ever.

We built Smriti with a hard constraint: zero external API calls for inference. Every LLM call, every embedding, every retrieval happens inside your GCP project on infrastructure that you own, with keys that only you control.

  • ✓
    Local LLM inference Ollama / vLLM runs inside your GKE cluster. No OpenAI. No Anthropic. No data in transit to external models.
  • ✓
    No model training on your data RAG-only architecture. Your data never enters model weights. Not ours, not anyone else's.
  • ✓
    Permission-aware retrieval Users only see answers grounded in content they already had access to in the source system. Private channels stay private.
  • ✓
    Encrypted at rest & in transit AES-256 encryption via GCP CMEK. TLS 1.3 in transit. OAuth tokens encrypted with Fernet in your database.
  • ✓
    Full audit trail Every query logged with user identity, timestamp, and sources accessed. Exportable for compliance.
