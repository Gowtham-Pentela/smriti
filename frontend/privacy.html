Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 6, 2026

The short version: Your organizational data is indexed and stored in your own GCP infrastructure. Smriti operators never have access to it. No data is ever sent to external AI APIs for inference. Ever.

1. Who We Are

Smriti is an organizational AI knowledge assistant. The product is deployed into the customer's own Google Cloud Platform (GCP) project. Gowtham Pentela, operating from Springfield, IL, USA, is the controller of this Privacy Policy for the software itself. Customers who deploy Smriti are independent controllers of their own organizational data.

2. Data We Collect and Process

Smriti collects two categories of data:

  • Organizational knowledge data: Messages, documents, meeting transcripts, and other content you authorize Smriti to index via OAuth or file upload. This data is stored in your Postgres database (pgvector) inside your GCP project. We have no direct access to it.
  • Product usage data: If you contact us for support, we may collect your name and email. We do not embed analytics trackers in the application.

3. Data Residency

All organizational data is stored exclusively in the GCP region you choose during deployment (default: us-central1). Data does not leave that GCP region or project boundary during normal operation. The GCP project is owned and billed to your organization, not to Smriti.

4. How We Use Your Data

Organizational data is used solely to provide the AI query service. Specifically:

  • Text is chunked and embedded using local models (Ollama / nomic-embed-text) running inside your cluster.
  • Embeddings and source text are stored in your pgvector database.
  • At query time, relevant chunks are retrieved and passed to a local LLM (running in your GKE cluster) to generate an answer. No external API call is made for inference.

We do not use your data to train any machine learning model.

5. Third-Party Data Processors

Smriti uses the following third-party services during normal operation:

  • Google Cloud Platform: Compute (GKE), database (Cloud SQL), and storage. Your GCP project is the controller. GCP's DPA applies. GCP DPA →
  • Slack: If you connect Slack, Smriti uses the Slack API to read authorized channel history. Slack's API terms apply. Only channels explicitly authorized during OAuth setup are read.

Smriti does not use OpenAI, Anthropic, Cohere, or any other external LLM API. Inference runs entirely on hardware inside your GCP project.

6. Access Controls and Security

  • Authentication: Google Identity-Aware Proxy (IAP) enforces Google SSO before any request reaches the application. No unauthenticated requests reach the backend.
  • Permission-aware retrieval: Users only receive answers grounded in content they already have access to in the source system (e.g., Slack channel membership). Private channels are indexed with restricted visibility flags.
  • Encryption at rest: All Cloud SQL data is encrypted using GCP-managed keys (CMEK option available). OAuth tokens stored in the database are additionally encrypted with AES-256 via Fernet.
  • Encryption in transit: TLS 1.3 enforced on all external traffic. Internal service-to-service traffic uses GKE Workload Identity.
  • Audit trail: Every query is logged with user identity, timestamp, and sources accessed. Audit logs are stored locally in your deployment.

7. Data Retention

Organizational data is retained until you explicitly delete it. You can clear the vector index at any time via the application UI (Clear Knowledge Index) or via direct database access. Cloud SQL automatic backups are retained for 14 days by default and can be configured in your Terraform deployment.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we process about you. For all data rights requests:

  • For data stored in your Smriti deployment (organizational data): contact your company's IT administrator, who controls your Smriti deployment.
  • For any personal data Smriti holds (e.g., support email): contact us at privacy@smriti.one. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Smriti is a B2B enterprise product not directed at individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will notify customers via the primary contact email on record at least 14 days before the change takes effect.
