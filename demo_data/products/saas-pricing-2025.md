# SaaS Pricing 2025 — Kiosk Underwriting

The kiosk underwriting product is sold to retail partners on a per-transaction basis. There are three pricing tiers, depending on the volume commitment from the partner.

## Tier 1 — Pay-as-you-go

- **Per-transaction fee:** ₹180 per decision rendered (auto-approve + manual review + decline all count as one decision).
- **Setup fee:** ₹50,000 (one-time, covers the integration of the partner's CRM and the partner's first 5 kiosks).
- **Minimum monthly commitment:** none. The partner can pause and resume the service.
- **Best for:** small retail chains (< 20 kiosks), new partners testing the product.

## Tier 2 — Committed volume

- **Per-transaction fee:** ₹140 per decision, for partners committing to at least 1,000 decisions per month.
- **Setup fee:** ₹25,000 (waived if the partner commits to 12 months).
- **Minimum monthly commitment:** ₹1,40,000 (i.e. 1,000 decisions × ₹140).
- **Best for:** mid-size retail chains and consumer electronics chains.

## Tier 3 — Enterprise

- **Per-transaction fee:** ₹95 per decision, for partners committing to at least 10,000 decisions per month.
- **Setup fee:** none.
- **Minimum monthly commitment:** ₹9,50,000.
- **Includes:** dedicated Customer Success Manager, 99.9% uptime SLA (vs 99.5% in the lower tiers), custom bureau pulls (e.g. integration with a partner-specific data source), and quarterly on-site business reviews.
- **Best for:** large retail chains and two-wheeler OEMs.

## What's included in every tier

- The kiosk hardware (tablet + card reader + printer) on a lease basis. The partner pays a refundable security deposit of ₹15,000 per kiosk.
- The Android app and all updates.
- The API access for the partner's CRM to pull decision status and disbursal status.
- The compliance updates (KYC, AML, bureau integrations) — these are pushed by Vibe and do not require partner action.
- Standard support: 9 AM to 9 PM IST, 7 days a week, with a 4-hour response SLA for P1 incidents.

## What is NOT included in any tier

- On-site installation and training (quoted separately, typically ₹8,000 per kiosk per visit).
- Custom UI changes (e.g. partner branding on the kiosk screen) — quoted separately.
- Custom reporting beyond the standard dashboard.
- Loan portfolio buy-out or co-lending arrangements.

## Recent changes

- 2025-12-15: Added Tier 3 (Enterprise) pricing in response to the Bajaj deal. Bajaj is the first Enterprise customer.
- 2025-09-01: Reduced Tier 2 per-transaction fee from ₹160 to ₹140 in response to competitive pressure from KreditBee.
- 2025-04-01: Added the 4-hour P1 response SLA to Tier 1 and Tier 2 (was 8 hours previously).
